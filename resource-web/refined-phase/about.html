Hello hello!

Welcome to my archival project — a way for me to digitally document my collection of precious stones.

Rocks have always been a major special interest of mine from a very young age. I am specifically fascinated by the fact that no single rock is made alike — each one possesses its own unique fingerprint. There is something so inherently human about that; the fact that the passing of time and environmental conditions can transform a rock entirely. Every fissure, jagged edge, or sudden colour shift is a physical record of where it has been and what it has endured.

This archive is my attempt to document and catalogue my personal collection that will, hopefully, continue to grow. I hope you enjoy exploring my website and I encourage you to start your own collection, even if it is not rocks.

A project from Melissa Andrade.
